HOW TO PLAY

🎮 Controls

  • Drag / Touch anywhere on screen to move your fighter jet
  • Auto-fires heat-seeking missiles continuously
  • Tap PAUSE button (top-right) to pause mission

🛡 Health System

Your jet has 3 Shield Bars. Each collision or enemy bullet removes one shield. Lose all shields and your aircraft is destroyed. Collect Green Orbs to restore shields.

⚡ Power-Ups

  • ■ Shield Restore - Replenishes 1 shield bar
  • ■ Rapid Fire - Doubles missile firing rate for 8 seconds
  • ■ Spread Shot - Fires 3-way missiles for 10 seconds
  • ■ Speed Boost - Increases movement speed for 6 seconds

🛫 Enemy Types

  • ◆ Stealth Fighter - Fast, zig-zag pattern, 2 HP
  • ▲ Delta Drone - Very fast, erratic, 1 HP
  • ▬ Heavy Bomber - Slow, massive, 5 HP, appears every 30s
  • ◉ Boss Aircraft - Level-end guardian, massive HP

⭐ Scoring & Stars

Earn up to 3 Stars per level based on score, kills, and shields remaining. Higher levels unlock advanced weaponry and faster aircraft.

🚀 About the Game

Air Combat Elite is a browser-based aerial combat arcade game built with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript. Take control of your aircraft, complete challenging missions, defeat enemy aircraft, collect powerful upgrades, and progress toward intense boss battles.

The game combines fast-paced aerial combat with a dynamic combat-style HUD, progressive mission system, power-ups, visual effects, and parallax environments — all playable directly in your browser without requiring an installation.

🏆 Key Features

  • 9 Progressive Missions — Progress from basic training to Ace Combat.
  • 4 Unique Power-Ups — Shield, Rapid Fire, Spread Shot, and Speed Boost.
  • 4 Enemy Types — Stealth Fighter, Delta Drone, Heavy Bomber, and Boss Aircraft.
  • 3-Star Rating System — Earn ratings based on your combat performance.
  • Dynamic Visual Effects — Afterburner flames, contrails, missile smoke, and explosions.
  • Parallax Environment — Clouds and terrain move at different speeds for a deeper sense of motion.
  • Progress Save — Your progress is stored locally in your browser using localStorage.
